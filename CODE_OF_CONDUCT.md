# Code of conduct

Courtnotes exists to support people doing accountability work in the courts. We want this project
to be welcoming to volunteers, organizers, lawyers, researchers and developers alike.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and kind, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and accept constructive
feedback gracefully. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Two project-specific expectations:

- **Never share real court-watch records** or anything that identifies a defendant, a volunteer or a
  victim in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Discuss judges and courts on the basis of data, not personal attacks.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/lobojoel2003/Courtnotes/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches maintainers confidentially) or by opening a minimal issue asking to be contacted.
Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially.
